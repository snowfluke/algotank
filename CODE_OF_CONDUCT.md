# Code of conduct

This project uses the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html) as its code of conduct. The full text at that address applies to everyone who takes part: in issues, pull requests, discussions, and every other place of the project.

## Summary

The game is made for children and for people who do not program, so the people who build it treat each other with the same care.

- Be kind and patient, and assume good intent.
- Give and accept feedback about the work, not about the person.
- Do not harass, insult or threaten anyone. Do not post sexual content or another person's private information.
- Keep the project a safe place for people of every age, background, body, identity and level of experience.

## Reports

Report a problem to the maintainer through the contact on the maintainer's GitHub profile ([snowfluke](https://github.com/snowfluke)). The maintainer keeps the name of the reporter private and decides on an action by the enforcement guidelines of the Covenant: correction, warning, temporary ban, or permanent ban.
